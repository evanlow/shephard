🛑 Irreversible Actions These operations permanently delete data and cannot be undone. Use only to reset the system before first live use. Type PURGE in each form to confirm.

Clear All Attendance

Deletes every attendance record across all events. Events and members are untouched.

Clear All Members

Deletes every member and all their attendance records. Groups and events are untouched.

Clear All Events

Deletes every event and all linked attendance records. Members and groups are untouched.

Clear All Groups

Deletes every custom group along with their events and attendance records. The ALL MEMBERS group is preserved.
